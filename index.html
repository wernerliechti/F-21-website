About me

I'm Werner and I bought my first camera in 2008. Satoshi published the Bitcoin whitepaper the same year. We were both just getting started. 18 years and a few hundred weddings later, I still get excited before every first dance. I came to Bitcoin in 2017 and haven't looked back since. I'm based in Burgdorf, Switzerland. I run on Coca Cola Zero. I cook, play piano (well) and banjo (my neighbours are very forgiving). When I'm not shooting weddings I'm occasionally driving a tractor during potato harvest on my parents farm. I shoot colourful, emotional images. Colour tells you how a moment felt. I accept Bitcoin because it's the right way to do business: private, direct, no middlemen.
